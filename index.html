01 — History

From NNA to NRE.

NRE carries forward the network, relationships, leadership systems, and expansion built during the NNA era while moving toward a broader multi-game identity.

NNA Era

Growth in Kiomet

NNA developed into a major Kiomet clan while strengthening relations with organizations including URE, RSL, and SBA and building a wider diplomatic network.

Network Era

SGN is created

Softbear Games Network was established during the NNA era to connect projects across Softbear games, later expanding beyond that original purpose.

Transition

NRE — New Republic Enterprises

The organization moved into the NRE identity, retaining its broader ideals while creating room for new rules, projects, games, clans, and expansion.

Current Era

NRN and the NRE Empire

SGN became the New Republic Network as the network expanded beyond Softbear. The NRE Empire now provides an umbrella for NRE-controlled and connected operations across multiple games.
